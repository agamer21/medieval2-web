NORMAN PROLOGUE

Your first battle

Click a blue regiment or its card. Shift-click or shift-drag selects more. Right-click the ground to move; right-click red troops to attack. Before the battle begins, right-click your half of the field to place regiments.

On touch: tap a card, then tap the ground or an enemy. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. WASD / arrow keys pan. Space pauses.

Keep archers behind infantry. Cavalry hits hardest at the charge and fares poorly against spears — flank with it instead. Attack an enemy’s rear to weaken morale. Rally can bring wavering troops back once every 30 seconds.

This is an original browser battle prototype. Native Medieval II combat and campaign systems are still being researched.
